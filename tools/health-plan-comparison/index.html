How the estimate is calculated

Estimated net yearly cost = 12 monthly premiums + your estimated share of entered covered care − employer HSA deposits − (only when enabled) estimated tax savings on your own HSA deposits. The cost share uses the entered deductible, coinsurance, and out-of-pocket maximum. It is a simplified estimate, not an enrollment recommendation.

Limits, assumptions, and sources

For 2027, federal HSA contribution limits are $4,500 for self-only coverage and $9,000 for family coverage, including employer deposits. A qualifying person age 55 or older may have a separate catch-up contribution. Check current IRS rules and your plan. The limit changes over time.

Source: IRS Revenue Procedure 2026-24, published June 15, 2026; checked October 17, 2026.

HSA contribution eligibility, qualified medical expenses, and the tax treatment of account earnings depend on IRS rules. See current IRS Publication 969; checked October 17, 2026. The investment illustration is a hypothetical calculation, not a forecast or promise.

This tool does not account for every plan rule, copay, prescription, out-of-network amount, noncovered bill, family-member deductible, investment return, account fee, tax situation, or eligibility exception. For family coverage it treats the family as one total. Check your benefits guide, IRS guidance, and payroll treatment.
