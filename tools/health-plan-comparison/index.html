How the estimate is calculated

The estimate adds 12 months of premiums and your share of the entered covered-care cost. It then subtracts the entered employer HSA contribution and, when enabled, your estimated HSA tax benefit. This is a simplified deductible and coinsurance calculation, not an enrollment recommendation.

Bill timing check

Limits, assumptions, and sources

For 2027, federal HSA contribution limits are $4,500 for self-only coverage and $9,000 for family coverage, including employer deposits. A qualifying person age 55 or older may have a separate catch-up contribution. Check current IRS rules and your plan. The limit changes over time.

Source: IRS Revenue Procedure 2026-24, published June 15, 2026; checked October 16, 2026.
